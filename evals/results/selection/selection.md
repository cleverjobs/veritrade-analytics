## Rules

- **signal**: Candidates: the frozen /signal configs at the default lede scope. Filters: dev300 inversion rate <= 2% at the frozen operating point (n=300); the articles dev set must not fail the cap, meaning the 95% CI of its inversion rate reaches down to 2% (the freeze's clear-failure rule: at n=30 one inversion is about 5% of the calls); warm native p50 excluding fetch <= 500 ms at lede. Rank: dev300 F0.5 macro (primary, n=300), then articles dev F0.5, then lower latency.
- **analyze**: Candidates: the frozen /analyze configs (full scope, ordinal |score| >= t, sentiment-first). Filters: the same inversion rules as /signal; streamed TTFT p50 <= 1.5 s and complete p50 <= 4 s (warm, native, excluding fetch). Rank: dev300 F0.5 macro, then articles dev F0.5, then lower latency.
- **summary**: Candidates: the frozen /summary models. Filter: deterministic pass rate >= 0.8 on the dev articles; streamed TTFT p50 <= 1.5 s and complete p50 <= 4 s. Rank: Opus judge faithfulness, then judge coverage, then key-fact coverage (key-fact coverage leads while the judge is pending).
- **winner**: The best-ranked candidate inside every filter. When no accuracy-eligible candidate meets its latency budget, the Pareto knee (metrics.knee) of quality against budget utilisation (the worst measured metric divided by its limit) among the measured candidates, with the reason stated.
- **bundle**: R8: the per-endpoint winners when their model files total <= 10 GB (decimal), else the combination of accuracy-eligible candidates that fits with the fewest rank steps away from the winners (ties: signal, then summary, then analyze keeps its better pick; then the smaller total).
