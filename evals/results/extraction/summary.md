# Extraction matrix

Generated by `uv run python -m veritrade_evals.extraction_bench report` from local measurements; no article text is stored here.

- Data: the 90 committed articles (all splits) from 10 sources; stored HTML through the serving `veritrade.extract.registry.extract`; third-party extractors bring their own parser (parser column `(built-in)`).
- Quality: macro means over articles against the Opus gold body. Tokens are lowercased `\w+` runs; P/R/F1 are bag-of-words with multiplicity; a failed extraction scores 0. Leakage is the share of extracted tokens outside every 5-token window that also occurs in the gold body (successful extractions only). 95% CIs: bootstrap over articles (B=1000).
- Time: wall ms of one `extract` call (parse + extract), median of 5 runs per article after a warm-up, then p50/p95 over articles, failures included.
- Tokens: chars / 4 of the uncapped `full` input (title + body) at p50; lede = the `/signal` lede input at the decision model's budget.
- Install: decimal MB of the RECORD files a row adds to the backend core (its closure without any parser or extractor); own strategies add 0 beyond their parser.
- Signal: `clef-flash:9b-q8_0` v4 (grid best), served sentiment at p >= 0.8; agreement = share of articles whose verdict on the row's lede equals the verdict on the gold lede, title held at the gold headline; a failed extraction is a miss.
- Machine: Apple M4 Pro, 48 GB, Python 3.13.12; Ollama had a model resident in 90/90 per-article samples (another eval was running), mean 1-min load average 6.1.

## Notes

- The gold body is a subset of the `strip-tags` lines (selectolax blocks) chosen by Opus, so `strip-tags` recall is about 1 by construction and own strategies share the gold's text joining; scores are bag-of-words and the leakage only needs 5 tokens in order, so segmentation is moot.
- The gold packets folded the page outside the span the served `selectolax/cascade` found (Opus could open the full page), so the gold leans toward the cascade; read own vs third-party gaps as upper bounds.
- The globenewswire, prnewswire and yahoo profiles were tightened after a first measurement with these articles in view, so their scores, and the overall means they feed, are optimistic; `.context/docs/design/03-extraction.md` keeps the first-run numbers.
- Timings come from one process on a shared workstation; see the environment block in matrix.json for load average and Ollama residency during the run.
- The `jsonld` strategy reads JSON-LD from the raw HTML and never builds the tree, so its 4 rows differ only by timing noise.
